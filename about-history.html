About TWG

History

Three companies, one full envelope contractor

The Worx Group (TWG) brings together three specialist companies under one roof: Roofworx, Woodworx and Fire-worx. Each business has its own trade, its own skilled teams and its own reputation. Together they give clients something few contractors can: one point of contact for roofing, cladding and waterproofing, carpentry and joinery, and fire doors, across the whole building envelope.

We are based in Bristol and deliver projects nationwide. Working as one group means fewer interfaces for main contractors and developers, clearer responsibility on site, and teams who already know how to work alongside each other.

The same values run through all three companies: high quality workmanship, the best materials for every job, and a professional, friendly service from people who take pride in what they build. As one client put it: “I would have no hesitation in recommending The Worx Group, they have demonstrated high quality workmanship & professionalism.”

Roofworx (UK) Ltd is where the group began. The company was started 11 years ago and built on 20 years of roofing experience. Since then it has earned an outstanding reputation for high quality workmanship using the best materials for every job. From its base in Bristol, Roofworx first built its name across the South West of England and now covers every aspect of roofing, all held to a very high standard of workmanship and customer service.

Our roofs are built to last and guaranteed, protecting the buildings and the people inside them for a long time to come. Roofworx is a TrustMark registered business, and TrustMark has assessed our work and given it their seal of approval. We also hold full Employers Liability Insurance.

We pride ourselves on a professional service and a friendly team who will take on any job, however big or small. On site, clients notice the attitude as much as the finish. One said: “The three roofers from Roofworx have been a great help and worked long days to push on the work. Any instructions given to them regarding cleanliness, PPE and safety they have reacted to with a smile on their faces.”

Delivering nationwide

What started in the South West is now a nationwide operation. Roofworx delivers projects across the country, from Glasgow, Sheffield, Coventry, London and Plymouth, working with national contractors and developers on large, technically demanding roofing and waterproofing packages. Whether it is a single home or a multi-storey development, every project gets the same attention to detail, the same quality of materials and the same commitment to finishing on time.

Woodworx are carpentry and joinery specialists based in the South West. They provide high quality trade services to domestic and commercial customers, both locally and in the surrounding areas. With a team of over 55 highly skilled carpenters, Woodworx has extensive experience in the construction industry and has tackled a wide range of challenging building needs over the years.

Carpentry and joinery are the core of the business, but the team also has a wide knowledge of building and maintenance work. Woodworx has always collaborated with skilled trade professionals to give clients a thorough and reputable service they can trust.

Woodworx is committed to bespoke carpentry and joinery that is both functional and good to look at. It uses the highest quality materials and close attention to detail to exceed clients’ expectations. The portfolio includes custom-made furniture, staircases, kitchen and bathroom renovations, built-in wardrobes and shopfitting. Each project reflects the client’s vision and style. We take our responsibilities seriously too: all timber and sheet materials come from PEFC and/or FSC certified suppliers.

Many of our clients come back to us again and again, and alongside its own work Woodworx increasingly works hand in hand with Roofworx, so clients can bring envelope and carpentry packages together under one group. One said: “Highly recommended, continuing as client for further works with Woodworx. Knowledgeable, effective and hardworking - couldn’t ask for more.”

Fire-worx is the group’s fire door specialist. It provides supply, inspection, installation, maintenance, testing and standardisation services for fire doors in commercial and residential properties. Fire doors are among the most important parts of a building’s passive fire protection, and Fire-worx exists to make sure they are reliable and compliant, keeping people and buildings safe.

The team is made up of highly skilled professionals who are well versed in fire door regulations and industry best practice. Every door Fire-worx works on is expected to meet the highest standards of quality and safety. The company takes a complete approach: thorough inspection, careful installation, regular maintenance, rigorous testing and adherence to industry standards.

Fire-worx has completed numerous projects of varying scale and complexity, from residential buildings to commercial establishments and industrial facilities. Its focus is proactive fire safety and long-term client relationships built on trust and reliability, with the aim of being the go-to fire door specialist for clients who need to protect lives and property. As one Director of Nursing & Care Services said: “Very impressed with the overall look of the doors supplied by Fire-Worx. Lots of compliments on site from the staff and the outcome is very positive.”
