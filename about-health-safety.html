Health & Safety

Safety without compromise

Safety on every project, every day

Our top priority on every project is the health, safety and wellbeing of people, not only our own operatives but anyone who may be affected by our work on site.

Health and Safety Management

At The Worx Group we pride ourselves on our excellent H&S record and are continuously striving to improve this area of our business. We have worked with a dedicated H&S Advisor for many years, who helps us go above and beyond in ensuring our operatives and those impacted by our work are safe.

Our Independent Health and Safety Advisors

We work with the same independent firm of health and safety consultants across all of our projects. Their advisors are members of IOSH (the Institution of Occupational Safety and Health) and bring over 30 years of combined experience in health and safety, including construction and the trades, so we always have competent, professional advice to hand.

Our advisors carry out regular site visits and safety inspections on our live projects. On each visit they walk the site to identify anything that is not compliant, review the documents held on site and check that work is being carried out in line with the current risk assessments and method statements. After every visit they compile a detailed report covering their findings and recommendations, so any actions can be closed out quickly and lessons are shared across all of our sites.

A Positive Safety Culture

Roofing and construction are hazardous industries. We keep our sites safe through a positive team culture where everyone takes responsibility for safety, and we are committed to continually improving our performance in line with client and public expectations.

RAMS and Toolbox Talks

Every job has its own Risk Assessments and Method Statements (RAMS), compiled by trained and competent persons. Our Site Managers carry out regular toolbox talks so that all site installers are kept up to date with the risks, methods and safety requirements on each project.

Our Operatives

All our operatives hold valid CSCS cards, which are regularly checked. All our site managers and supervisors have SMSTS and SSSTS qualifications respectively, and at least one First Aider is allocated to each site.

The Same Standard for Everyone

Anyone working on a project under The Worx Group is expected to meet the same health and safety standards as our own teams.

Certificates and Accreditations

  • SMAS Worksafe certificate
  • CHAS accreditation

Both meet the SSIP core criteria recognised by the HSE, demonstrating an effective H&S management system.
