Station Street, Stratford

Hawthorne House

Second-fix joinery for RG Group on purpose-built student accommodation and a sixth-form college for Unite Students, across levels B2 to 36. Woodworx installed approximately 1,868 student and 426 college doorsets with ironmongery, plus skirting, architrave and window boards, with fire doorsets recorded under the BM TRADA Q-Mark scheme via Fire-Worx.

Old Sneed Park

A residential refurb for Stonewood Builders. Woodworx stripped the existing pitched roof and built a new timber structure, laid free-issue Petersen clay tiles to the roof and walls, fitted Velux roof windows and balconies, VM Zinc Pigmento Red standing seam and approx. 145m² of cedar cladding.
